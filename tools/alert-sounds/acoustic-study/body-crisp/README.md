# Lower body, crisper attack — listening comparison

[Listen](low-high-ab.wav): **low A, low B, high A, high B**.

A is the soft acoustic study. B uses lower-register recordings to give the body weight,
with a short, separate recorded strike adding definition. Low combines medium-struck
marimba with a quiet dry wood attack; high combines lower vibraphone notes with a short
hard-mallet strike. There are no bass oscillators, synthetic sweeps, distortion, pitch
shifts, or added reverberation. Both designs target -20 dBFS active RMS; this is electrical
level matching, not a claim of equal perceived loudness.

These comparison copies remain outside Android resources. A forms the basis of Timber and B
forms the basis of Ember, with longer production arrangements. Sources and CC0 terms are in the parent folder. Reproduce with `render_body_crisp.py`; append `--check` to verify
source hashes, exact output, headroom, DC offset and zero endpoints. Python/NumPy and
macOS afconvert are required. Explicit alarm sound choices are retained; unset app defaults now use Ember.

Production update: these short audition WAVs remain unchanged. The selectable Timber and Ember
collections now use longer arrangements with restrained linear mastering; they are no longer byte-identical
to these study copies. Ember is the default for unset sounds; explicit choices remain saved.
